# Tic-tac-toe

A game for two people sharing one device.

## Language

**Player**:
One of the two people playing, represented by X or O.

**Board**:
A three-by-three arrangement of nine squares where players place their marks.

**Mark**:
An X or O placed by a player.

**Turn**:
One player's opportunity to place a mark.

**Win**:
Three of one player's marks in a row, column, or diagonal.

**Draw**:
A full board with no winning row, column, or diagonal.

**Game**:
A sequence of alternating turns, beginning with an empty board and ending in a win or draw.
