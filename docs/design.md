# Game design

Design interview complete. Approved for implementation.

## Agreed choices

- A standalone game for fun.
- Two people take turns on the same device.
- One self-contained HTML file, opened by double-clicking.
- Lily's pink and blue palette, using soft pastels, dark readable text, and distinguishable Xs and Os.
- Standard 3×3 rules: X starts, turns alternate, three matching marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins; a full board without a winner is a draw.
- Occupied squares are unavailable. Moves stop after a win or draw.
- Each new game starts fresh with X. No scorekeeping.
- No sounds. A subtle mark animation and highlighted winning squares.
- Visible turn and result text. A “New game” button available throughout, resetting the board even during an unfinished game.
- Pink Xs, blue Os, and a pale background.
- A phone-friendly layout supporting touch, mouse, and keyboard. Shapes and text communicate player identity and results independently of colour.

These choices are easy to reverse and do not warrant an ADR yet.
